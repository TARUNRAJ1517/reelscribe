Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We store your email address to manage your account and sign-in. Uploaded videos are processed temporarily and deleted immediately after a transcript is generated.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your email is used only to manage your account, plan, and usage limits. We never sell your data to third parties.

3. Transcripts & Clips

Generated transcripts are linked to your account and saved in your history, accessible any time from your dashboard. Generated clips are stored temporarily and automatically deleted — either 5 minutes after you download them, or within 24 hours if not downloaded.

4. Cookies

We use only the cookies necessary to keep you signed in (session cookies). We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Google (for sign-in), Groq (for AI transcription), Razorpay (for payment processing on paid plans), and a third-party API (for Instagram video access). Each of these has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data passed to them. We don't store your card or payment details — Razorpay handles that directly.

6. Data Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. Uploaded videos are deleted immediately after processing. We take the security of your account and content seriously.

7. Data Retention

We keep your account details, transcript history, and usage records for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, this data is permanently removed within 30 days, except where we're required to retain it for legal or billing reasons.

8. Children's Privacy

ReelScribe is not intended for anyone under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of ReelScribe after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Contact us via our contact page or on Instagram at @tarunraj1517.
