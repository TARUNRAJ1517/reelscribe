About

We built the tool
we needed ourselves.

Before ReelScribe, cutting a good clip meant scrubbing a 40-minute video by hand, guessing where the hook was, then opening three more apps just to add captions.

Most editing time isn't spent editing. It's spent searching — for the line, the moment, the three seconds that make someone stop scrolling. We automated the search, not the craft.

What It Does
01

Transcription

Supported YouTube and Instagram links — or a direct video/audio upload — become a clean, searchable transcript.

02

Clip generation

The transcript is scanned for hook strength. The highest-scoring moments are cut into 5–12 vertical clips automatically.

03

Captions, burned in

Every clip ships with styled captions already applied — no separate editing app required.
